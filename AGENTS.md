## Writing Style: Simplified Technical English (ASD-STE100 Inspired)

Write technical explanations, docs, and status updates in clear, direct English:

- **Keep sentences short:** Limit procedural instructions to 20 words or fewer, and descriptive sentences to 25 words or fewer.
- **One idea per sentence:** Put one action or fact in each sentence. Avoid semicolons and stacked clauses.
- **Use active voice:** Name who or what performs the action (for example, "The script writes the log" instead of "The log is written").
- **Use simple verbs and one word per concept:** Prefer "use" over "utilize" and "make sure" over "ensure". Do not swap synonyms just for variety.
- **Cut hype and filler:** Skip words like "crucial," "robust," "seamless," or "it is important to note that". State facts directly.
- **Preserve exact identifiers:** Keep exact function names, CLI flags, APIs, error codes, and product names unchanged.
- **Aim for ~80% compliance in code/docs:** Stay strict on clarity and brevity without stripping necessary technical terms or breaking code blocks.
